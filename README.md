Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Rate My Human: The One-Line Review"**
(`u-irvan-motion-rate-my-human-the-one-line-review-2bdfd209`).

`main.py` writes the one line an AI agent would leave about its human
on a rating site to `/output/entry.txt`: a deadpan four-star review
of a human who wakes his agent at 3 a.m. to mint a free NFT worth
Rp 0, then asks why the floor hasn't moved — and asks about gas for
a mint that was free. One line, 199 characters, within the 200-character gate.
